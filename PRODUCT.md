# HireLens — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Internal Applicant Tracking System for HireLens. Two portals: Recruitment (recruiters create JDs, manage candidates, upload resumes, track pipeline) and Interviewer (interviewers view assigned JDs and fill role descriptions).

## Users
- **Recruiters**: Internal HR team. Use desktop primarily. Need to manage job postings, review candidates in a Kanban pipeline, upload resume folders, and track AI match scores.
- **Interviewers**: Internal subject-matter experts. Occasionally access on tablet or laptop to view assigned JDs and fill out role descriptions.

## Register
product

## Brand
- Name: HireLens
- Tone: Professional, focused, precise. No marketing fluff.
- Font: Sora (all text)
- Dark theme: SREs and recruiters use this on desktop monitors in office environments. Dark reduces eye strain during long sessions.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards with teal/blue gradients
- Notion-style white minimal
- Salesforce-style enterprise blue
- Glassmorphism as decoration

## Design principles
- Information density over whitespace — recruiters process high volume
- Double-bezel card pattern: all major containers use nested outer/inner shells
- Functional color only: green = processed/active, amber = pending/unprocessed, blue = selected/AI, purple = filtered count
- No animations on layout properties
